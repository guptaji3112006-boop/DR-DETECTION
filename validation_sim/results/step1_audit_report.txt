========================================================================
  8. PREPROCESSING COMPARISON: TRAINING vs INFERENCE
========================================================================

Training script (train_model.py):
  1. tf.io.read_file + tf.image.decode_png(channels=3)  → RGB uint8
  2. tf.cast(float32) / 255.0  → [0, 1]
  3. tf.image.resize([224, 224])
  4. Augmentation: random_flip, brightness(0.15), contrast(0.9-1.1), saturation(0.9-1.1)
  5. No tf.clip_by_value after augmentation

Inference (model_adapter.py):
  1. PIL Image.open → resize(224,224) → convert('RGB')  → RGB uint8
  2. np.asarray(float32) / 255.0  → [0, 1]

Model architecture:
  EfficientNetB3 — need to verify if it has an internal Rescaling layer
